This file contains ready-to-use copy for short-form videos and the first itch.io project page. Keep every clip honest to the gameplay currently shown in the recording. Do not add fake player counts, invented scores or gameplay that is not in the live build.

## Short-form video formats

Create vertical 9:16 screen recordings with readable subtitles. Aim for 15–25 seconds. Start with the hook immediately; avoid long logos or intros. Show real HUMANITY gameplay and finish with the play link on screen.

### Video 1 — Seven questions, one daily run
- **0–2 sec (hook):** “Seven geography questions. One official run. How far can you get?”
- **2–7 sec:** Show the Atlas Daily intro and start button.
- **7–15 sec:** Show a real question, timer and a submitted answer.
- **15–20 sec:** Show the result screen and the Practice Lab button.
- **End card:** “Play Atlas Daily free — HUMANITY”.
- **Caption:** “A new shared geography challenge every day. How many would you get right?”

### Video 2 — You can keep guessing
- **0–2 sec (hook):** “This is not multiple choice.”
- **2–12 sec:** Show a real Atlas Daily question and two genuine attempts while the timer is running.
- **12–17 sec:** Show the accepted-answer feedback.
- **End card:** “Seven questions. Twenty-five seconds each. Play free.”
- **Caption:** “Type what you know. Atlas Daily checks your answer against its approved answer set.”

### Video 3 — Beat the Atlas Orb
- **0–2 sec (hook):** “Would you know an answer before the fuse runs out?”
- **2–12 sec:** Show a real Globe Rush category, the live fuse and the Orb passing after a valid answer.
- **12–18 sec:** Show the room continuing and the player list.
- **End card:** “Globe Rush — live geography multiplayer in HUMANITY.”
- **Caption:** “The countdown is shared. Every answer matters.”

### Video 4 — Challenge your friends
- **0–2 sec (hook):** “Your group chat says they know geography?”
- **2–8 sec:** Show the private-room creation flow and a room code. Blur or replace any real personal information.

### Video 5 — Two ways to play

### Video 6 — After your daily run

## Posting rhythm

Start with two or three good clips each week rather than flooding every platform with near-identical posts. Publish the same core recording as YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, but adjust the caption and opening line to the platform. Make a simple spreadsheet of publish date, opening hook, views, average watch time, completion rate and profile/link clicks. Review after two weeks and remake the formats that actually hold attention.

Use only relevant tags, for example:
- #Geography
- #GeographyQuiz
- #TriviaGame
- #MultiplayerGame
- #BrowserGame

Keep the play URL in the channel/profile link and repeat a short spoken or on-screen call to action. Do not promise that a clip will go viral; compare results and improve the hook.

## itch.io page — prepared copy

**Important publishing gate:** do not publish an empty placeholder page. itch.io's discovery indexing expects a public project, a cover image, and a page that offers a playable browser build or a download. A description-only external link is not a completed playable listing. First prepare and test a browser-playable package that can connect to the production HUMANITY API, then upload it and verify guest play, account sign-in, multiplayer rooms and reconnects from the itch.io embed origin.

### Suggested project title
HUMANITY: Daily Geography & Multiplayer Quiz

### Short description
A free browser geography game with a seven-question daily challenge and a live multiplayer answer race. Explore the world, discover rare answers and challenge your friends.

### Full description
**One world. Two ways to explore.**

HUMANITY is a free geography game platform built around quick thinking, real answers and a shared world of questions.

**ATLAS DAILY**
- Seven questions shared by players around the world each UTC day.
- Twenty-five seconds for each question.
- Open-answer gameplay: type an answer instead of choosing from a list.
- One official daily run, with Practice Lab and World Archive available for more play.
- Discovery Points, daily streaks and global rankings.

**GLOBE RUSH**
- Live multiplayer geography rounds with the shared Atlas Orb and countdown.
- Quick Play, Team Rush, Survival and private rooms.
- Create a private room for 2–10 players and invite friends.
- Ranked matches and seasonal progress for signed-in players.

**EXPLORER PROFILES**
- Account XP, levels, achievements, play history and planet avatars.

Play in your browser. No separate game client is required.
