YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

HUMANITY uses essential browser storage for sign-in and gameplay. Optional first-party usage analytics help improve the site. They are off unless you allow them; no advertising or cross-site tracking is used.

HUMANITY · SITE STORAGE

Cookie policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Cookies used by HUMANITY

HUMANITY sets one first-party cookie, humanity_cookie_notice, after you dismiss the notice. It remembers that the notice has been seen for up to six months. It is not used to identify you for advertising or to track you across websites.

Browser storage

The site also uses local browser storage for essential game operation: a guest identifier, your sign-in session token, and your sound, sound-volume and motion preferences. These items stay in this browser until they are cleared or your session expires. They are not advertising cookies.

Analytics and advertising

Optional first-party usage analytics are off by default and run only after you choose “Allow analytics”. When enabled, HUMANITY records page names and aggregate usage events using a random browser token that is hashed by the API. The analytics table does not store IP addresses, user-agent strings, account IDs, submitted answers or private room messages. Optional analytics records are retained for up to 365 days. You can change your choice at any time using Privacy preferences in the footer.

Third-party services

The interactive globe library is loaded from jsDelivr. When your browser requests that library, the CDN receives normal connection information. Google or Discord only receive information when you choose one of those sign-in options.

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing the HUMANITY session token signs you out on this device.

HUMANITY · YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Information used to run the games

Guest play uses a random browser identifier so the daily challenge can recognise an attempt from this device. If you create an account, HUMANITY stores your username and account details. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes, not as readable passwords.

For sign-in with Google or Discord, HUMANITY receives the account identifier and the profile information those services return, such as a verified email address and avatar where available. Your first successful social sign-in creates a HUMANITY account; a verified matching email may connect it to an existing account.

Gameplay and community data

HUMANITY processes daily challenge attempts and scores, rankings, experience, streaks, Globe Rush scores and ratings, friend connections, and live room activity. Live room chat is used to deliver messages to the people in that room.

Why and where information is used

This information is used to provide sign-in, save progress, operate multiplayer rooms, calculate rankings, prevent duplicate daily attempts, and maintain the service. The website's API and database are hosted using Render and Neon PostgreSQL.

Sharing and tracking

HUMANITY does not display advertising or use third-party analytics trackers. Optional first-party usage analytics require your explicit choice and are used only to understand aggregate page usage. The random visitor token is hashed server-side, is not linked to your HUMANITY account, and is automatically removed after 365 days. You can opt out at any time through Privacy preferences in the footer. HUMANITY does not sell account information.

Account controls

You can sign out from your account menu. Signing out revokes the active server session. A self-service account deletion control and a dedicated privacy contact are not yet published on this version of HUMANITY.

This is a plain-language product notice based on the features currently implemented. Before opening the service to the public, the site owner should add a privacy contact and have the notice reviewed for the countries where HUMANITY will be offered.
