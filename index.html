ABOUT HUMANITY / 04

Understand more.
Assume less.

HUMANITY is an independent exploration of the data that describes our shared world. Its purpose is simple: make global evidence easier to find, compare and question.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Clarity without false certainty.

Global statistics are useful, but they are not the whole truth. A national average can hide differences by income, age, gender, region and opportunity. We show the measure, its source and its year so you can understand what a number does — and does not — tell you.

HUMANITY is designed as a public-facing data explorer. It is not affiliated with the World Bank, the United Nations or any national government.

A NOTE ON INTERPRETATION
