HOW IT WORKS

A new geography challenge every UTC day

Every day, players around the world receive the same seven-question edition. Questions are designed for typed answers rather than multiple-choice guessing, so you can enter a place or fact you genuinely know. The daily edition resets at 00:00 UTC.

1. Read the clue

Each question gives you a geography or world-culture prompt. Type an answer that matches the approved answer set.

2. Make it count

You have 25 seconds for each question. You can submit more than one guess while the timer is running, and one optional hint is available for each question.

3. Discover your score

Accepted answers earn Discovery Points. Rarer answers can earn more, and the global daily rankings show completed account runs.

ATLAS DAILY FAQ

Questions before you play?

Is Atlas Daily free?

Yes. Atlas Daily is available to play free in your web browser. You do not need to download a separate game client.

Can I play more than once in a day?

You get one official daily attempt per UTC day. After that, you can still play Practice Lab packs and previous editions in the World Archive.

Do I have to get an answer on the first try?

No. You can submit further guesses while the 25-second timer is active. The game checks answers against its approved answer set.

When does the next challenge arrive?

The shared daily edition resets at 00:00 UTC. All players receive the same date-based edition.

Do practice scores count towards the official ranking?

No. Practice Lab and archived editions are for replay and learning; they do not change official daily rankings.
